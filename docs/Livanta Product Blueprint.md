# Livanta

### *One place to manage the things that keep your life running.*

**Product category:** Everyday Solutions / Smart Utilities
**Market:** Nigeria → Africa
**Product type:** Mobile-first life-management platform
**Core promise:** **Know what needs attention. Know what's coming. Stay ahead.**

---

# 1. THE PRODUCT IN ONE SENTENCE

Livanta is a personal and household life-maintenance platform that helps Nigerians organize responsibilities, track important dates and expenses, manage documents and assets, and proactively identify what could become a problem before it does.

---

# 2. THE PROBLEM

Everyday Nigerian life involves dozens of recurring responsibilities: rent, electricity, internet, school fees, vehicle maintenance, insurance, driver's licence, passport, generator servicing, appliance repairs, subscriptions, household tasks, family commitments.

The information is scattered across WhatsApp, SMS, email, bank apps, paper receipts, notebooks, calendars, and memory.

The result: forgotten renewal → missed deadline → penalty → emergency expense → stress.

Livanta brings these responsibilities into one place and adds proactive intelligence.

---

# 3. THE BIG IDEA

Most apps ask: "What do you want to record?"
Livanta asks: **"What could become a problem if you don't act?"**

---

# 4. PRODUCT PILLARS

# 9. "WHAT'S ABOUT TO BECOME A PROBLEM?" ENGINE

The signature feature. Continuously looks at the user's information and determines what is overdue, expiring, approaching, recurring, unusually expensive, missing, or potentially forgotten — then converts raw information into actionable alerts.

Example (Document): Driver's licence expires December 15.
- 90 days → "Your driver's licence expires in 3 months."
- 30 days → "Your driver's licence expires next month."
- 7 days → "Your driver's licence expires in 7 days."

---

# 10. RISK PRIORITIZATION

- 🔴 Urgent — action required now.
- 🟠 Important — action should happen soon.
- 🟡 Upcoming — start preparing.
- 🟢 Routine — no action needed yet.

---

# 11. SMART ALERTS

Instead of: "Reminder: rent."
Livanta says:
> 🏠 **Rent is due in 14 days.** Your last recorded rent amount was ₦1,200,000. Have you started preparing for it? [Mark as prepared] [Remind me later]

---

# 12. LIFE PREPARATION MODE

When an important event approaches, Livanta creates a checklist.

Example (Rent, due in 30 days): Confirm rent amount · Confirm payment details · Review service charges · Set money aside · Contact landlord/agent · Save payment evidence.
